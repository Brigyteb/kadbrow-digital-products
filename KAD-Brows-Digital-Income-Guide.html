The build
Five steps, in order.
01
Set the foundation
Write your brand book — voice, audience, services and products. It's what makes everything Claude creates sound like KAD.
02
Start small
Film a short at-home lamination course on your phone and sell it with your KAD kit. Five to eight quick lessons on a simple course platform.
03
Build the ladder
Add your masterclass, pro certification and 1:1 mentorship — then your highest-ticket piece: a pre-recorded online nano brow certification.
04
Open the Inner Circle
Once around 30 people are interested, launch the membership. One technique video a week and a monthly live call keep the recurring income coming.
05
Turn on marketing
Record your webinar, cut it into weekly content, run a small ad, and connect Gmail, Canva and your email platform to Claude.
